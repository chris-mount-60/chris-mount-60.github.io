Sticky reflected Brownian motion spends a positive amount of time at 0, though its zero set contains no interval. The talk shows how to sample its position and its local time at 0 exactly, and how to refine one path at any times in any order. It accompanies the paper of C. Mount and I. Fatkullin, *Exact bridges for sticky reflected Brownian motion* (to appear on arXiv).
