Reflected sticky Brownian motion is built from reflected Brownian motion by a time change that slows the clock at 0 in proportion to the local time there. The talk constructs the process this way, recalls the exact law of its position at a fixed time, and then samples its bridges: given the position and the local time at two times, it samples both at any time in between, exactly. Joint work with Ibrahim Fatkullin.
