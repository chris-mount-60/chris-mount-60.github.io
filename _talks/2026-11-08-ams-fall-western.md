Sticky reflected Brownian motion spends a positive amount of time at 0, though its zero set contains no interval. The talk shows how to sample its position and its local time at 0 exactly, from a few uniform, normal and exponential variables, and how to refine one path at any times in any order. It accompanies the paper of C. Mount and I. Fatkullin, *Exact bridges for sticky reflected Brownian motion* (to appear on arXiv).

The [pages and movies of the talk](https://chris-mount-60.github.io/sticky-bm-bridge-presentation/) include two interactive pages. In [One path, refined](https://chris-mount-60.github.io/sticky-bm-bridge-presentation/refine/) you query a bridge by clicking on it, with the samplers of the paper running in the browser. [The zero set of reflected Brownian motion, and its local time](https://chris-mount-60.github.io/sticky-bm-bridge-presentation/zero-set/) shows the approximations of the zero set and of the local time closing in on their limits.
