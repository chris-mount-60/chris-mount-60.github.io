\section{Research Interests}
% ===================================================================
Probability and stochastic processes; diffusions with sticky and reflecting
boundary behavior; interacting particle systems with sticky interactions;
Brownian local time and Ray--Knight theory; exact simulation and Monte Carlo
methods for diffusions.
