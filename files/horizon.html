The picture is not to scale: heights are drawn about times larger than distances, so that the curve is visible. The numbers are computed at full scale with an Earth radius of 3959 miles, and they ignore the slight bending of light by the air. With your eye above the water, the rule is applied twice: your line of sight first drops to the horizon, a distance D away, and the tower is then hidden by 8(d − D)2 inches.
